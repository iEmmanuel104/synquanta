# Ad copy — "You see it every week. No black box."

**Role:** how we do it · **Audience:** warm / retargeting · **CTA button:** Learn More
**Landing page:** `https://synquanta.com/process`
**Creative:** `../out/how-we-work/`

This concept answers the objection, so it is the one to retarget with. Someone
who has already seen `what-we-build` knows what we make; what stops them
signing is not knowing what the months in between look like.

**The differentiator is visibility, not speed.** Nothing here may imply "fast",
and no line may put a duration on research, design or delivery.
