# Ad copy — "Bring us the hard part"

**Role:** direct response · **Audience:** warm, bottom of funnel · **CTA button:** Contact Us
**Landing page:** `https://synquanta.com/contact`
**Creative:** `../out/hard-part/`

This is the only concept that asks for something. Run it against people who
have already seen one of the other two — it converts badly cold, because it
assumes you know who is asking.

Do not swap the CTA to *Get Quote*. The site's own position is that a price
comes after a written plan, and a Get Quote button promises the opposite.
