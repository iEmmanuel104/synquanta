# ERP campaign — scripts + storyboard **v2**

**Status: DRAFT v2, awaiting founder approval.** No voice or credit spend until it is approved. v1 is kept in `SCRIPT-v1.md`.

## What changed from v1 (two independent reviews)

**Adopted**
- **Positioning thread across every asset:** *"Your business isn't standard. Your software shouldn't be either."* Supporting line: *"One connected system. Built around your business."* We sell **custom** ERP, not the ERP category. The word "custom" appears early in every cold ad.
- **The master hook targets growing businesses**, the buyers who can afford a custom build: *"Your business has grown. Has your system grown with it?"*
- **Unsupported claims removed:** "chases it for you", "never loses a follow-up", "as it happens / real-time". Every benefit is now *visibility and connection*, matching the site copy (`src/constants/services.ts`).
- **Process renamed** to **Understand → Design → Build → Deploy**. Training stays in the voiceover ("get your team running on it"); the founder confirmed it is always included.
- **New cold ad E, Custom vs generic.** It answers "why not just buy Odoo, SAP or Dynamics?"
- **Real interface footage:** 2–3s of **EstateOS screens** (our own design, not client IP), labelled "Designed by SynQuanta". Never presented as a live product with users.
- **Earlier brand:** a subtle mark from frame 1. Cold ads name SynQuanta by about 5s, and the master by about 20s.
- **Timing fixed:** v1 had 175 words, which is 75s with *no* pauses. The master is now **about 155 words at about 85s**.
- **CTA:** states **"free 30-minute process review"** explicitly.
- **4:5 layouts:** recomposed, never cropped from 9:16.
- **Funnel staging and tracking gate:** see §5.

**Not adopted**
- Reviewer 2's praise of "chases it for you" as benefit language. It breaks the claims guardrail.
- Reviewer 2's "Quanta naming collision". It is unverified, and the custom positioning already differentiates us.
- Reviewer 1's "scheduled 7-day review". That was created in their tool, not in this workspace. A 7-day read is planned in §5 anyway.

**Ground rules**
- **Voice:** calm, warm, neutral international, about 140 wpm with real pauses.
- **Claims:** site-copy capabilities only. No client names, metrics or prices. Modules are described as examples, never as "included in every project".

## 3. Cold ads A–E: 15s each (9:16 + 4:5), prospecting

There are five *distinct* concepts. Each one says "custom ERP" and "SynQuanta" by about 5s, and they share a 2.5s end card: *Free 30-min process review · synquanta.com*.

**A — Memory (owner pain).** *Launch wave 1.*
> **VO:** Your business shouldn't depend on one person's memory. Spreadsheets, chat groups, disconnected teams. SynQuanta builds a custom ERP around how you work. Free 30-minute process review: synquanta.com.
> **On-screen:** **Your business shouldn't depend on someone's memory.**
> **Visual:** one figure holding a towering stack of sticky notes. The stack wobbles, and the notes flow into the hub.

**B — Visibility (finance pain).** *Launch wave 1.*
> **VO:** The invoice went out. The follow-up didn't. When your numbers live in five places, you can't see them. A custom ERP puts them in one. SynQuanta builds yours.
> **On-screen:** **How much of your business can you actually see?**
> **Visual:** an invoice slips into the gap between two tools. The pieces (invoices, expenses, reports) then slide onto one dashboard tile.

**C — Imagine (education).** *Launch wave 1.*
> **VO:** What if your whole business ran on one connected system? Sales, inventory, finance and people, sharing the same information. That's an ERP, and SynQuanta builds yours around how you work.
> **On-screen:** **What if your whole business ran on one system?**
> **Visual:** a giant "ERP?" splits into five nodes, which join into the hub.

**D — Different businesses (persona).** *Wave 2.*
> **VO:** Different businesses. Different workflows. One thing in common: they need systems that work together. Inventory, clients or properties, SynQuanta builds the custom ERP around yours.
> **On-screen:** **Different businesses. One thing in common.**
> **Visual:** three 2s cuts (warehouse → client desk → building with EstateOS UI), each visually distinct.

**E — Custom vs generic (objection).** *Launch wave 1. This is the differentiator.*
> **VO:** Your business has its own way of working. Why force it into software that wasn't built for you? SynQuanta designs and builds custom ERP systems around your business.
> **On-screen:** **Your business isn't generic. Your ERP shouldn't be either.**
> **Visual:**
> 1. A workflow (coloured blocks) is jammed into a rigid grey template, and the blocks bend and misalign.
> 2. The template dissolves.
> 3. A purpose-built frame forms around the workflow's real shape, and the blocks click in.

Each script is about 28–33 words.

## 4. Style + production

- **Look:** flat, rounded vector illustration in the house style of `public/illustrations/custom-erp.svg`. Problem scenes are busy and slightly jittery; solution scenes are still and aligned. Keep the motion simple, because the contrast is the message. It is a story, not a software tutorial.
- **Palette** (`marketing/lib/brand.mjs`):
  - forest greens for structure (`#1B4332`, `#2D6A4F`);
  - sage and mint accents (`#52B788`, `#95D5B2`);
  - off-white and cream backgrounds (`#F8FAF9`, `#D8F3DC`);
  - one muted coral, used for the problem accent only.
- **Type:** Clash Display for headlines and Inter for captions. Captions are at most 2 lines and at least 48px at 1080w.
- **Real UI:** EstateOS boards in `~/Desktop/Projects/estateos/design-system/screens/`:
  - `34 Console - Leads pipeline reservations payments`;
  - `12 Public site - Estates units`;
  - `42 Customer portal - Login instalment receipts`.
  Render them through Chrome, then animate a slow pan inside a device frame. Use demo data only; the data pack is fictional.
- **Higgsfield-style shots H1–H3 on fal.ai:** texture only, with no text, UI or faces. They sit behind the HTML at 40–60% opacity.
- **Layouts:** 16:9, 9:16 and a **recomposed** 4:5. The 9:16 safe zone is 270px top, 670px bottom, 65px sides.
- **Audio:** an ambient bed at −24 to −20 LUFS, with a −14 LUFS integrated master.
- **Provider:** fal.ai for both the video shots and the ElevenLabs voice. The `FAL_KEY` is read at run time from Railway `umbrelar` and never written into this repo. A per-shot cost estimate comes first.
- **Order:** style frames and voice samples go for approval first. Only then render full-length.

## 5. Campaign staging + launch gate

| Stage | Audience | Assets | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Awareness | Broad, country-split reporting | Ads **A, B, C, E** (D in wave 2) | Recognise the problem; see custom ERP as an option |
| 2. Consideration | Video viewers 25%+, site visitors, engagers | 30s cut, 85s master | Trust in the approach and the team |
| 3. Conversion | Engaged and warm visitors | Direct "free 30-min process review" ad | Booked reviews |

- **Priority segments:** distributors and wholesalers, manufacturers, real estate, growing service firms and multi-location businesses.
- **Priority roles:** owners, MDs, COOs, operations managers and finance directors.
- **Geography:** global, reported **by country** in Ads Manager.

**Pre-launch tracking gate.** This is a website change that needs separate approval and `sq deploy website`.
- Already live: the PostHog `contact_form_submitted` event and the Meta `Lead` event fire on a successful submission (`src/components/sections/Contact.tsx`).
- **Missing:**
  1. `?need=erp` should pre-select "A custom ERP system" on the form.
  2. UTM parameters should be captured with the submission, so each enquiry maps to an ad.
  3. The pipeline from enquiry to qualified to sale should be logged, using the existing `ContactSubmission` table.
- **Later:** a dedicated `/erp` landing page, which is a separate plan.

**Judge on:**
- cost per **booked review** and per qualified enquiry, not views;
- a 7-day read after launch: hook rate, hold rate, CTR, then lead quality.
