## The v3 rule: write for a business owner, not a software person
A distributor in Lagos, a property firm in London or a services company in Toronto should understand every line **without knowing what "ERP" means**.

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| custom ERP / connected system | one system for your whole business, built around how you work |
| inventory | stock |
| procurement | buying |
| reporting | reports |
| workflows / processes | how your business runs |
| lead | enquiry |
| record / books | information / accounts |
| deploy | launch, and help your team get started |
| financial operations / visibility | your money / see it all in one place |
| process review | tell us about your business, or book a session |

- **"ERP" stays, but it is never the first word.** It always comes *after* the plain description: "…businesses call this an ERP." It also appears once on every end card ("Custom business systems (ERP)"), so people who search the term recognise us.
- **The test before approval:** show a cut to 3–5 business owners who know neither SynQuanta nor the term ERP. Ask them:
  1. What does SynQuanta do?
  2. What problem does it solve?
  3. What makes it different?
  4. What do I get if I contact them?
  If anyone needs help answering, simplify further.

**CTA, the same everywhere (v3.1, 2026-11-13).** Voiceover: "Visit synquanta.com to tell us about your business, or book a session with our team." On screen: **Tell us about your business · synquanta.com/erp**. No phone call and no time length: the goal is the website, where the `/erp` questionnaire tells us who they are before we talk.

## 3. Cold ads A–E: 15s each (9:16 + 4:5), prospecting

Each ad names SynQuanta by about 5s. They share a 2.5s end card: *One system. Built around your business. · Tell us about your business · synquanta.com/erp*.

**A — Memory.** *Wave 1.*
> **VO:** Your business shouldn't depend on one person's memory. Spreadsheets, chat groups, teams that don't talk. SynQuanta builds one system around how you work. Start at synquanta.com.
> **On-screen:** **Your business shouldn't depend on someone's memory.**

**B — See your money.** *Wave 1.*
> **VO:** Your invoices are in one place. Your expenses are somewhere else. How do you see the full picture? SynQuanta builds one system that brings it together.
> **On-screen:** **How much of your business can you actually see?**

**C — Imagine.** *Wave 1.*
> **VO:** What if your whole business ran on one system? Sales, stock, money and staff, all in one place. That's an ERP, and SynQuanta builds yours around how you work.
> **On-screen:** **What if your whole business ran on one system?**

**D — Different businesses.** *Wave 2.*
> **VO:** A warehouse. A services company. A property business. Different work, same problem: information everywhere. SynQuanta builds one system around how each one works.
> **On-screen:** **Different businesses. Same problem.**

**E — Built for you, not generic.** *Wave 1. This is the differentiator.*
> **VO:** Your business has its own way of working. Why force it into software that wasn't built for you? SynQuanta builds business systems around the way you work.
> **On-screen:** **Your business isn't generic. Your software shouldn't be either.**

**Timing:** each 15s ad gives about 12.5s to the voiceover before the 2.5s end card. At a calm 140 wpm that is about 29 words, and every script above is within that (checked). If a read runs long, the end card fades in under the last spoken line rather than the read being rushed.

The visuals are the same as in v2 §3: the sticky-note stack, the invoice slipping into a gap, "ERP?" splitting into icons, three industry cuts, and the rigid template dissolving. On-screen icons replace words wherever possible (cart, box, coin, person, chart), which also keeps Meta's AI dubbing clean.

## 4. Style + production

- **Look:** flat, rounded vector illustration in the house style of `public/illustrations/custom-erp.svg`. Problem scenes are busy and slightly jittery; solution scenes are still and aligned. Keep the motion simple, because the contrast is the message. It is a story, not a software tutorial.
- **Palette** (`marketing/lib/brand.mjs`):
  - forest greens for structure (`#1B4332`, `#2D6A4F`);
  - sage and mint accents (`#52B788`, `#95D5B2`);
  - off-white and cream backgrounds (`#F8FAF9`, `#D8F3DC`);
  - one muted coral, used for the problem accent only.
- **Type:** Clash Display for headlines and Inter for captions. Captions are at most 2 lines and at least 48px at 1080w.
- **Real UI:** EstateOS boards in `~/Desktop/Projects/estateos/design-system/screens/`:
  - `34 Console - Leads pipeline reservations payments`;
  - `12 Public site - Estates units`;
  - `42 Customer portal - Login instalment receipts`.
  Render them through Chrome, then animate a slow pan inside a device frame. Use demo data only; the data pack is fictional.
- **Higgsfield-style shots H1–H3 on fal.ai:** texture only, with no text, UI or faces. They sit behind the HTML at 40–60% opacity.
- **Layouts:** 16:9, 9:16 and a **recomposed** 4:5. The 9:16 safe zone is 270px top, 670px bottom, 65px sides.
- **Audio:** an ambient bed at −24 to −20 LUFS, with a −14 LUFS integrated master.
- **Provider:** fal.ai for both the video shots and the ElevenLabs voice. The `FAL_KEY` is read at run time from Railway `umbrelar` and never written into this repo. A per-shot cost estimate comes first.
- **Order:** style frames and voice samples go for approval first. Only then render full-length.

## 5. Campaign staging + launch gate

| Stage | Audience | Assets | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Awareness | Broad, country-split reporting | Ads **A, B, C, E** (D in wave 2) | Recognise the problem; see a system built for them as an option |
| 2. Consideration | Video viewers 25%+, site visitors, engagers | 30s cut, 85s master | Trust in the approach and the team |
| 3. Conversion | Engaged and warm visitors | Direct "tell us about your business / book a session" ad | Questionnaires and sessions |

- **Priority segments:** distributors and wholesalers, manufacturers, real estate, growing service firms and multi-location businesses.
- **Priority roles:** owners, MDs, COOs, operations managers and finance directors.
- **Geography:** global, reported **by country** in Ads Manager.

**Pre-launch tracking gate.** This is a website change that needs separate approval and `sq deploy website`.
- Already live: the PostHog `contact_form_submitted` event and the Meta `Lead` event fire on a successful submission (`src/components/sections/Contact.tsx`).
- **Missing:**
  1. `?need=erp` should pre-select "A custom ERP system" on the form.
  2. UTM parameters should be captured with the submission, so each enquiry maps to an ad.
  3. The pipeline from enquiry to qualified to sale should be logged, using the existing `ContactSubmission` table.
- **Later:** a dedicated `/erp` landing page, which is a separate plan.

**Judge on:**
- cost per **booked review** and per qualified enquiry, not views;
- a 7-day read after launch: hook rate, hold rate, CTR, then lead quality.
