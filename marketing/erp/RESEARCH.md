# ERP campaign — research brief (Step 1)

Researched 2026-11-13. Most sources are agency or vendor blogs, not Meta's own documentation. Treat every number below as a starting hypothesis, then confirm it in Ads Manager.

## 1. What the research changes in our plan

| Plan assumption | What the research says | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| The 60–90s explainer is the main ad | Cold prospecting performs best at **6–15s**, warm consideration and retargeting at **16–30s**. Meta says under 15s tends to win in feed. | The **75s explainer is the master** for the website, YouTube, LinkedIn and retargeting. It is **not** the cold ad. |
| 2–3 × 15s hooks are minor variants | Andromeda ranks ads on **creative diversity**. It wants concepts that are meaningfully different: a different pain, persona or format. Re-skins of one concept count as one ad. | Make **4 conceptually distinct 15s ads**, each with a different pain or persona (below). This is not 4 openings stuck onto one video. |
| A calm, slow opening | Viewers decide in the first 1.5–3s. Motion in the first half-second, the problem first, no logo intro. | **The opening is crisp; the rest is calm.** Frame 1 is already moving and states the pain. The calm pacing starts from about 3s. |
| The voiceover carries the message | Most Meta viewing is **sound-off**. Captions must be burned in, and the visuals must tell the story without audio. | Burn on-screen text and captions into every frame. The voiceover is a second layer. |
| Abstract "ERP" illustrations | Abstract conceptual imagery underperforms, and concrete product demonstration wins. Animated workflows and before/after splits lift CTR. | Illustrate **concrete things**: an invoice, a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp follow-up, a pipeline board, a dashboard tile. Each scene needs a **before/after** turn. |

## 2. Deliverables (revised)

| Asset | Length | Ratios | Funnel role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Master explainer | ~75s | 16:9, 9:16 | Website, YouTube, LinkedIn, retargeting video viewers |
| Consideration cut | 30s | 9:16, 4:5 | Warm audiences (engaged with the site or the 15s ads) |
| Cold ads A–D | 15s each | 9:16, 4:5 | Prospecting, broad targeting, run together in one Advantage+ ad set |

The four cold concepts, deliberately different from each other:

- **A, Chaos (pain, owner):** "Your business runs on 14 spreadsheets and someone's memory." Shows the scattered mess, then a connected system.
- **B, Lost money (pain, finance):** an invoice that was never followed up. Shows the cash leaking in the gaps between tools.
- **C, "What even is an ERP?" (education, curious founder):** a plain-English definition in 15s.
- **D, Industry flip (persona):** the same pipeline story told for a distributor, a services firm and a real-estate developer (the EstateOS-style example), cut fast.

## 3. Structure for every cut

- **0–3s, hook:** motion in frame 1, the pain as text on screen, no logo.
- **3–15s, problem and payoff:** mess turns into a connected pipeline.
- **15–30s, proof:** how SynQuanta builds it (map → design → build → train) and one example.
- **End, CTA:** synquanta.com plus "Book a free process review". The logo appears here only.

The brand should still be identifiable early. A subtle Orbit Atom mark and the brand palette are present from frame 1, so the ad does not read as generic stock.

## 4. Specs

- **9:16:** 1080×1920 master. The safe zone is our existing **270px top / 670px bottom / 65px sides**. Sources give the unified March-2026 zone as 14% top and up to 35% bottom, which is about 269px and 672px, so our rule already matches.
- **4:5:** 1080×1350 feed.
- **16:9:** 1920×1080.
- **Audio:** about −14 LUFS. The music bed sits well under the voiceover.
- **Captions:** burned in, max 2 lines, large and high contrast, inside the safe zone.

## 5. Global audience

- **Master language:** English, with a neutral international voice (ElevenLabs).
- **Meta Advantage+ AI dubbing (2026):** re-dubs the ad audio into other languages and keeps the frames. Ads carry an AI-translation disclosure, and the supported language list needs checking in Ads Manager.
- **Implication:** keep on-screen text **short and language-light**, mostly icons, numbers and diagrams, so dubbed versions still read correctly. If a market proves out, render native-language text versions later.
- **Phase 1 targeting:** broad by country cluster, letting the creative do the targeting (the Andromeda model). Use English-speaking business markets first: NG, GH, KE, ZA, UK, AE, US, CA. Expand after the first read.

## 6. What to measure

| Metric | Weak | OK | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3s plays ÷ impressions) | <20% (Reels <25%) | 25–35% | 35%+ |
| Hold rate (15s plays ÷ 3s plays) | <25% | 40–50% | 60%+ |
| ThruPlay rate | <15% | ~22% | 30%+ |

How to read the results:

- **Low hook rate:** rework the first 3s.
- **Good hook, weak hold:** the middle sags.
- **Good watch time, low CTR:** the CTA or offer is weak.
- **Good CTR, poor leads:** the problem is the offer or targeting, not the video.

Judge the campaign on **cost per qualified enquiry**, not on views. Build retargeting audiences from people who watched 25% or more, and serve them the 30s cut and the explainer.
