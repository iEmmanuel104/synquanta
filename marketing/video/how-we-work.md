# Video kit — "You see it every week. No black box."

**Concept role:** how we do it · **Length:** 15s · **Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16)
**Opening frame:** `marketing/out/how-we-work/story.png` (Reels/Stories)
or `marketing/out/how-we-work/status.png` (WhatsApp Status)

**Two safe zones, not one.** Cut the video once at 1080x1920, but keep every
subtitle and every logo inside the *Reels* box, which is far tighter:

| Destination | Top | Bottom | Sides |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Status | 120px | 200px | 60px |
| IG/FB Stories + Reels | **270px** | **670px** | 65px |

Meta's caption, audio row and Learn More button occupy the bottom third of a
Reel, and the like/comment/share rail runs up the right edge. A subtitle placed
for Status disappears underneath all of it. Design to 270/670 and the same cut
plays correctly on both.

Subtitles burned in — most of this is watched with the sound off.

**No timelines.** The differentiator here is *visibility*, not speed — do not
let the edit imply "fast". Never put a duration on research, design or delivery.

## Script

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0s | **HOOK.** "You see it every week." then "No black box." in mint. | "You see it every week. No black box." |
| 3.0–7.0s | Cut: a whiteboard being worked, hands, sticky notes. | "We find out who your users are before we build for them, and settle the expensive decisions on paper." |
| 7.0–11.0s | Four steps stack in: Research → Design → Build → Launch and stay on it. | "Then we engineer it. And we're still there after it goes live." |
| 11.0–15.0s | Logo, `synquanta.com`. | "You'll have something to click through while there's still time to change your mind. SynQuanta." |

The closing line is the one that earns the click. Slow down for it.

## Shot list

1. **0–3.0s** Type card, two beats, hard cut between them.
2. **3.0–7.0s** Whiteboard mid-problem: arrows, a crossed-out box, sticky notes. Hands adding to it. Slow handheld pan. Must look genuinely unresolved, not a tidied set.
3. **7.0–11.0s** Motion graphic: four rows stack in, 120ms apart, connecting hairlines drawing between them. **No durations on screen.**
4. **11.0–15.0s** Logo on the dark hero gradient, URL fades up.

## Model prompts

Structure: Subject → Action → Environment → Cinematics. Present tense, 60–120 words.

### Shot 2 — Veo
> A whiteboard in a working office is covered in half-erased diagrams, arrows joining boxes, one box crossed out, sticky notes crowding the lower edge. A hand enters from the right, adds a single line, and pauses. The camera pans slowly left to right at eye height, handheld with a gentle natural wobble, 35mm, shallow depth of field so the far edge of the board softens. Cool daylight from a window off-frame left, muted greens and greys. Real and mid-problem rather than solved.

**Negative:** legible writing, readable text, brand logos, watermarks, tidy or staged whiteboard, people posing, warped hands, extra fingers.

### Shot 2 — Runway
> Handheld pan across a messy office whiteboard, half-erased diagrams and arrows, sticky notes, a hand adding one line. Muted green-grey palette, natural window light, subtle film grain, shallow focus, documentary feel. Unresolved and in progress rather than polished.

**Negative:** readable writing, logos, watermark, oversaturation, glossy corporate, fast motion.

### Shot 2 — Sora
> This is a problem before anyone has solved it. A whiteboard covered in arrows and half-erased boxes, one crossed out. A hand comes in, adds a line, hesitates. The camera drifts across the board the way someone would look at it while thinking. Daylight from a window off to one side. Nothing here is tidy, and that is the point of the shot.

**Negative:** legible writing, on-screen text, logos, staged perfection, people addressing the camera.
