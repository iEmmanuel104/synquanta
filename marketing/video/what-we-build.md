# Video kit — "The software your business actually runs on"

**Concept role:** what we do · **Length:** 15s · **Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16)
**Opening frame:** `marketing/out/what-we-build/story.png` (Reels/Stories)
or `marketing/out/what-we-build/status.png` (WhatsApp Status)

**Two safe zones, not one.** Cut the video once at 1080x1920, but keep every
subtitle and every logo inside the *Reels* box, which is far tighter:

| Destination | Top | Bottom | Sides |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Status | 120px | 200px | 60px |
| IG/FB Stories + Reels | **270px** | **670px** | 65px |

Meta's caption, audio row and Learn More button occupy the bottom third of a
Reel, and the like/comment/share rail runs up the right edge. A subtitle placed
for Status disappears underneath all of it. Design to 270/670 and the same cut
plays correctly on both.

Subtitles burned in — most of this is watched with the sound off.

**No timelines.** Do not say how long anything takes. Scope drives the schedule
and the schedule is agreed per project, in writing.

## Script

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0s | **HOOK.** The line, white on forest green, hard cut. | "Most businesses run on software somebody else built for somebody else." |
| 3.0–6.5s | Cut: hands at a keyboard, a build coming together on screen. | "We build the version that fits yours." |
| 6.5–11.0s | Four capabilities stack in, mono numerals. | "Web platforms. iOS and Android. The internal tools that don't come off a shelf. And AI, where it actually earns its keep." |
| 11.0–15.0s | Logo lockup, `synquanta.com`. | "Designed and engineered by one team, first sketch to launch. SynQuanta." |

**Read it flat.** The claim carries itself; performing it makes it sound like
every other agency ad.

## Shot list

1. **0–3.0s** Static type card, slow 1.02× push. Legible within 300ms — nothing animates in.
2. **3.0–6.5s** Over-shoulder at a desk: hands on a keyboard, monitor glow, on-screen content out of focus. Shallow depth of field.
3. **6.5–11.0s** Motion graphic over dark green: four rows stack in, 120ms apart, mono numerals `01`–`04` in sage.
4. **11.0–15.0s** Logo on the dark hero gradient, orbit node completes one pass, URL fades up.

## Model prompts

Structure: Subject → Action → Environment → Cinematics. Present tense, 60–120 words.

### Shot 2 — Veo
> A developer's hands rest on a mechanical keyboard in a dim studio, typing in short bursts then pausing. A monitor fills the background, its content thrown well out of focus into soft blocks of light. A desk lamp throws warm key light from the upper right; the ambient fill is cool and green. The camera holds a low three-quarter angle at 50mm and performs a slow 15cm push-in, shallow depth of field at f/1.8 with focus locked on the hands. Calm, unhurried, documentary realism.

**Negative:** readable text, readable code, on-screen UI detail, brand logos on keyboard or monitor, watermarks, extra fingers, warped hands, stock-photo gloss.

### Shot 2 — Runway
> Close low-angle shot of hands typing on a mechanical keyboard, dim studio, monitor glowing far out of focus behind. Warm key light from upper right against cool green ambience, film grain, very shallow focus, slow push-in. Understated and editorial, not corporate.

**Negative:** readable text, code, logos, watermark, oversaturation, fast cuts, lens flare.

### Shot 2 — Sora
> This is what building actually looks like: not a montage, just someone typing in short bursts and then stopping to think. We stay low and close on the hands, the monitor a soft wash of light behind them, the room dim apart from one warm lamp. The camera drifts in slowly enough that you barely notice. Nothing on screen is legible and nothing is performed.

**Negative:** readable text or code, brand logos, people addressing camera, stock-footage polish.
